Why Conformal Prediction? A raw 65% ADHD probability is ambiguous — should a teacher act? Conformal prediction gives a mathematically rigorous answer by outputting a prediction SET rather than a point estimate. Coverage guarantee: (1−α) = 90% on the held-out calibration set. This prevents over-referrals based on uncertain predictions.
